ABOUT ME

HELLO MY NAME IS
MUHAMMAD ZAIDAN
PRIMA AL GHIFARI

You can call me Zaidan or Ghifar. I am a Visual Communication Design student (Diploma program) at Sebelas Maret University (UNS). I have a keen interest in the world of design, particularly in graphic design, branding, and illustration. I am a highly curious individual with a constant desire to grow. For me, design is not merely about art; rather, it is a way of communicating our thoughts through digital artistic media.
